# 🎵 CloudBeats

Welcome to **CloudBeats**! This is your ultimate music companion that lets you stream your entire music library directly from your Microsoft OneDrive. No need to worry about storage limits on your phone—just upload your favorite tracks to OneDrive and stream them on the go!

## ✨ Features

- **☁️ Stream from OneDrive:** Access your personal music library securely through Microsoft OneDrive.
- **🎧 Offline Playback:** Going somewhere without internet? Download your favorite songs with a single tap and listen anywhere!
- **📜 Smart Playlists:** Have `.m3u` or `.wpl` playlist files in your OneDrive? CloudBeats will automatically detect them and import your playlists!
- **✔ Multi-Select:** Long-press on any song to enter multi-select mode. Easily add multiple songs to a playlist in one go!
- **🖼️ Album Art:** Enjoy beautiful album covers right in the player. CloudBeats pulls high-quality album art directly from your OneDrive metadata.
- **🔄 Over-the-Air Updates:** Never miss a new feature! Go to `Settings -> Check for Updates` to easily download the latest app versions directly from GitHub.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Install the App:** Download the latest version of CloudBeats from the [Releases](https://github.com/dhanu/cloud-beatsxx/releases) page.
2. **Sign In:** Open the app and log in securely with your Microsoft account.
3. **Upload Music:** Ensure your music files (and playlists!) are inside the `spotify_downloads` folder on your OneDrive (or you can configure this in Settings).
4. **Sync & Enjoy:** Pull down to refresh your library, and start listening!

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Where should I put my playlist files?**
A: You can place `.m3u` or `.wpl` files in the same OneDrive folder as your music, or in a subfolder like `playlists`. The app will find them automatically during a sync!

**Q: How do I update the app?**
A: Open the app, go to Settings, and tap **Check for Updates**. If a new version is available, you can download and install it right away.

**Q: What music formats are supported?**
A: We support all standard audio formats including `mp3`, `aac`, `flac`, `ogg`, `wav`, `m4a`, `wma`, and `opus`.
